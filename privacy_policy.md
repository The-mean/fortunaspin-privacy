# FortunaSpin Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2024

## 1. Introduction

FortunaSpin ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our FortunaSpin mobile application (the "App").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- Wheel configurations and settings
- App preferences (theme settings, sound settings)
- User-created content (wheel titles and options)

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- Device information (model, operating system version)
- App usage statistics
- Crash reports
- Ad interaction data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the App
- Save your wheel configurations and preferences
- Improve the App's performance and user experience
- Show relevant advertisements
- Generate anonymous usage statistics

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All wheel data and preferences are stored locally on your device
- No personal data is transmitted to our servers
- App usage data and crash reports are collected through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics
- Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob

## 5. Advertisements

Our App uses Google AdMob for advertising:
- AdMob may collect device information and usage data
- Ad personalization can be controlled through your device settings
- For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Children's Privacy

- Our App is not intended for children under 13
- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13
- If you are a parent and discover your child has provided us with information, please contact us

## 7. Your Choices

You can:
- Use the App in offline mode to disable ads
- Clear local data through your device settings
- Opt out of analytics collection
- Control ad personalization through device settings

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
- Updating the "Last updated" date
- Notifying you through the App

## 9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- Email: support@fortunaspin.com
- Website: https://fortunaspin.com

## 10. Compliance

This App complies with:
- Google Play Store policies
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
